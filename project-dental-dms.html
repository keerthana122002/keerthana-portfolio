Project Snapshot

Patient Record Management

Centralized system to store and manage patient case sheets, reports, and treatment history.

Role-Based Access

Secure login for doctors, staff, and admin with controlled access to sensitive dental data.

Workflow Automation

Streamlined approval and document flow between departments and clinical units.

Real-Time Tracking

Track document status, updates, and activity logs for better accountability.

Design Goals

Clinical Efficiency

Enable quick access to patient records for faster diagnosis and treatment decisions.

Data Security

Ensure privacy of patient information with strict authentication and authorization.

User-Friendly Interface

Simple and intuitive UI for doctors and staff to manage records with ease.

Digital Transformation

Reduce manual paperwork and improve efficiency through digital record handling.
